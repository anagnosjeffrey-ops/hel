What to announce
Limits

Leave blank for no limit. The app tells you once when you reach one.

Reading the symbols

Reading the numbers works offline with no key. Reading the actual symbols on the reels needs an Anthropic API key, which is stored only on this phone and sent only to Anthropic.

Camera

How to use this

  1. Sit at the machine and press Start narrating. Allow the camera when asked.
  2. Press Aim the camera and turn the phone until the guide tone pulses fast, then it chimes. Prop the phone up facing the screen.
  3. Play normally. Each time the reels stop, you will hear a sound and then the result.
  4. Press Read the board any time to hear the symbols.
  5. Press How am I doing for your running total.

Sounds: rising chime means a win, and the more notes the bigger it was. A short low blip means no win. A two-tone alarm means the machine has gone out of frame and you should press Aim the camera again.

Works offline after the first run. Check the rules where you play before pointing a camera at a machine.
